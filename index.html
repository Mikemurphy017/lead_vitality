FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. Each lead goes to one business only. On Growth and Dominate plans you can also lock exclusive territory, so no competitor on Lead Vitality™ gets leads in your ZIP codes.

What makes a lead "high-intent"?

A high-intent lead has an active need, a near-term timeline and a budget that fits your service, and has confirmed they want to be contacted. We qualify all three before a lead reaches you, then rank it with the Vitality Score™.

What if I get a bad lead?

Wrong numbers, duplicates, out-of-area requests and services you don't offer can be flagged from your dashboard for credit.

Do you work with regulated industries like law, finance and insurance?

Yes. Campaigns for attorneys, financial advisors and insurance agents use consent-based opt-ins and compliant language. You're responsible for your own professional advertising rules, and we'll match our messaging to your compliance requirements.

How fast can I start getting leads?

Most territories can launch within a few business days of onboarding, once your service area, qualification questions and delivery settings are set up.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month. You stay because the leads work.
